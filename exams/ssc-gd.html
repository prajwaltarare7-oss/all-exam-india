About SSC GD

The recruitment process can include a computer-based examination and further stages such as physical tests, medical examination and document verification. The exact sequence and standards are controlled by the current official notification.

What to practise

  • General intelligence and reasoning
  • General knowledge and general awareness
  • Elementary mathematics
  • English or Hindi language practice
  • Physical and medical standards must be checked separately in the official recruitment notice.

Suggested preparation approach

Practise all four written-test areas consistently. Use timed mocks to improve question selection and accuracy. For physical stages, rely on the current official standards and safe, age-appropriate preparation guidance.
