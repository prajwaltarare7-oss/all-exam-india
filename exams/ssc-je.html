What to practise

  • Civil Engineering: core concepts and numerical problem practice
  • Electrical Engineering: circuits, machines, measurements and fundamentals
  • Mechanical Engineering: mechanics, thermal science and machine-related fundamentals
  • General intelligence and reasoning
  • General awareness and current developments.

Suggested preparation approach

Choose only the technical branch relevant to your application. Revise core concepts, solve numerical problems step by step and maintain a formula/error notebook. Compare every practice topic with the latest official syllabus.

Who may use this page?

Diploma- and degree-level engineering candidates preparing for notified SSC Junior Engineer posts, subject to post-specific eligibility.

Practice-material note: SSC EXAM.IN provides AI-generated original practice material. These sets are not official SSC previous-year question papers and do not guarantee selection. Use the latest official SSC notification for eligibility, syllabus, exam stages, dates and marking rules.

Frequently asked question

Can one SSC JE mock cover every branch equally? No. Civil, Electrical and Mechanical have different technical syllabi. Select and practise the branch relevant to your application.
