About SSC CHSL

The Combined Higher Secondary Level examination is designed for candidates applying to notified posts with the required higher-secondary qualification. Post-specific conditions and skill or typing requirements must be confirmed from the latest official notice.

What to practise

  • English language: grammar, vocabulary and comprehension
  • General intelligence: analogy, series, classification and logical reasoning
  • Quantitative aptitude: arithmetic and basic mathematical concepts
  • General awareness: important events and core static topics
  • Typing or data-entry skill requirements, if applicable to the post, need separate practice.

Suggested preparation approach

Practise short topic-wise sets, then combine subjects under a timer. For posts with a typing or data-entry requirement, maintain a separate skill-practice routine and follow the exact requirements in the notification.

Frequently asked question

Is SSC CHSL the same as SSC CGL? No. CHSL is associated with notified higher-secondary-level posts, while CGL is for graduate-level posts. Always check the qualification and post details in the official notice.
