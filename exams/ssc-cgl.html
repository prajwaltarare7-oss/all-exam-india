About SSC CGL

The Combined Graduate Level examination is intended for recruitment to a range of graduate-level roles. Post requirements and examination stages can vary by the current SSC notification, so always read the official notice for the post you choose.

What to practise

  • Quantitative aptitude: arithmetic, algebra, geometry and data interpretation practice
  • Reasoning: verbal and non-verbal problem solving
  • English: vocabulary, grammar and reading comprehension
  • General awareness: history, geography, polity, economy and science
  • Post-specific or additional papers, where applicable, should be checked in the latest notice.

Suggested preparation approach

Build accuracy before speed. Practise one subject at a time, note the questions you get wrong, and then attempt a timed mixed mock. Review explanations after every attempt instead of only checking the score.
