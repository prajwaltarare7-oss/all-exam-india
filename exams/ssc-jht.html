About SSC JHT

Preparation requires strong command of both Hindi and English, including comprehension, grammar, vocabulary and accurate translation. Check the current notification for the exact post, educational requirements and paper scheme.

What to practise

  • Hindi-to-English translation practice
  • English-to-Hindi translation practice
  • Hindi language and grammar
  • English language and comprehension
  • Vocabulary, usage and precise meaning in context.

Suggested preparation approach

Translate short passages in both directions and compare meaning, tone and accuracy. Build a subject-wise vocabulary list and practise comprehension regularly. Avoid literal translation when it changes the intended meaning.

Frequently asked question

Is SSC JHT preparation only about memorising vocabulary? No. Translation accuracy, comprehension, grammar and the ability to preserve meaning are also important.
