About SSC Selection Post

There is no single syllabus that fits every Selection Post vacancy. Candidates must match the post code, region, essential qualification and subject requirements in the official notice before choosing a practice level.

What to practise

  • Matriculation-level practice for vacancies requiring that qualification
  • Higher Secondary-level practice for 10+2 vacancies
  • Graduation-level practice for graduate vacancies
  • Subject-specific knowledge where the post requires it
  • Post code, region and qualification checks before applying.

Suggested preparation approach

Start with the exact post code and required education level. Compare the official syllabus with your preparation topics, then practise the closest matching level. Do not assume one mock pattern applies to all vacancies.

Who may use this page?

Candidates applying to a specific SSC Selection Post vacancy across Matriculation, Higher Secondary or Graduation levels.

Frequently asked question

Is every SSC Selection Post exam identical? No. The level and subject requirements depend on the notified post and its essential qualification.
