What to practise

  • Numerical and mathematical ability: arithmetic fundamentals
  • Reasoning ability and problem-solving
  • General awareness: current events and static knowledge
  • English language and comprehension
  • For Havaldar vacancies, review the separate physical requirements in the official notice.

Suggested preparation approach

Strengthen basic arithmetic and reasoning first. Use short timed practice sessions and review each mistake. Keep general awareness revision regular, and do not confuse a practice score with official qualifying criteria.

Who may use this page?

Candidates preparing for SSC Multi-Tasking Staff recruitment and notified Havaldar vacancies.

Frequently asked question

Does every SSC MTS candidate have the same additional physical test? Not necessarily. Check the current notice and the post applied for to understand any additional requirements.
